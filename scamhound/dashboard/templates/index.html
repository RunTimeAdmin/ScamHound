Risk Score Disclaimer

ScamHound's risk scores are generated through automated analysis of on-chain data, token holder distribution, liquidity metrics, and AI-powered pattern recognition. These scores are intended as informational tools only and should not be considered financial advice.

  • Legitimate token structures may appear high-risk. Some tokens are intentionally designed with concentrated holdings for valid reasons such as project treasuries, marketing allocations, development funds, or founder vesting schedules. A high risk score does not necessarily indicate fraudulent intent.
  • Always verify independently. Before making any decisions, review the token's official documentation, website, roadmap, and community channels to understand the intended token distribution and purpose.
  • Scores reflect a point-in-time snapshot. Token metrics change rapidly. A score generated now may not reflect the token's current state.
  • Not financial advice. ScamHound is a research and monitoring tool. Users are solely responsible for their own investment decisions. We do not recommend buying, selling, or holding any token.

By using ScamHound, you acknowledge that automated risk analysis has inherent limitations and agree to conduct your own due diligence.
